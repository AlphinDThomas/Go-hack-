// Problem Statement

The Deepfake
Crisis in Identity Verification

Traditional KYC systems rely on a single point of verification — a selfie or a video call — that is trivially defeated by AI-generated deepfakes and pre-recorded video injection. Billions in financial fraud are executed this way every year.

As generative AI becomes commoditized, the attack surface for synthetic identity fraud expands exponentially. Existing passive checks like document OCR or single-frame liveness are no longer sufficient.
