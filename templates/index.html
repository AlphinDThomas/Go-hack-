// Problem Statement

The Deepfake
Crisis in Identity Verification

Traditional KYC systems rely on a single point of verification — a selfie or a video call — that is trivially defeated by AI-generated deepfakes and pre-recorded video injection.

As generative AI becomes commoditized, the attack surface for synthetic identity fraud expands exponentially. Existing passive checks like document OCR or single-frame liveness are no longer sufficient.

🎭

Deepfake Video Injection

Attackers route AI-synthesized face-swap video directly into the camera pipeline, bypassing any purely visual check.

📼

Pre-Recorded Video Replay

Looped footage of a real person is replayed to bypass time-agnostic liveness checks. Undetectable without physiological signals.

🤖

Adversarial AI Models

GAN-driven face generators now produce photorealistic sequences that fool conventional depth and texture-based detectors.
